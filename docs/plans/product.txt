- Keep the product header clean, uncluttered, and aligned.
- Give the gear button a consistent outline, border radius, focus ring, and hover state.
- Provide the accessible label `Product actions`.
- Set `aria-expanded="false"` and associate the control with the action drawer.

### Open state — drawer expands toward the left

When the gear button is clicked:

1. Rotate the gear smoothly as the drawer opens.
2. Expand a horizontal contextual action drawer from the gear toward the left.
3. Position the drawer so it visually opens behind the Back to Products button when the two occupy overlapping space.
4. Keep the Back to Products button above the drawer in the visual stacking order, with its click behavior fully preserved.
5. Reveal three clearly identifiable actions:
   - Edit Product.
   - Duplicate.
   - Delete Product.
6. Use icons and text labels for all three actions.
7. Apply a restrained surface shadow, consistent border, and appropriate spacing.
8. Keep all action labels readable and clickable.

**The drawer must open leftward from the gear, not toward the right.** The animation must feel like a horizontal drawer being pulled out from behind the header controls, not like a standard dropdown menu opening downward.

The drawer must not obscure the product content unnecessarily or cause the header layout to jump.

Use a suitable positioning and stacking strategy so that the action panel expands smoothly while the Back to Products button remains accessible and visually in front wherever they overlap.

### Close behavior

The gear must behave as a genuine toggle.

- Clicking it while open closes the drawer and reverses the gear rotation.
- Clicking outside the drawer closes it.
- Pressing Escape closes it.
- Selecting an action closes the drawer before or as the action begins.
- When the drawer closes, return to the compact default header state.

Do not leave the drawer open after navigation.

### Animation specifications

Use the existing animation utilities or CSS transitions if available.

Target an approximately 200–300 ms interaction.

The gear may rotate about 90–180 degrees during opening, then return smoothly during closing. Choose the rotation amount that feels natural and keeps the icon visually clear.

Use a restrained combination of:

- Gear rotation.
- Horizontal drawer expansion or translation.
- A subtle opacity transition.
- Appropriate easing.

The movement should feel connected: the drawer opens as the gear rotates and retracts as the gear returns.

Do not use continuous spinning, exaggerated bouncing, large scaling effects, or animation delays that slow down the user.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by reducing or removing nonessential animation.
